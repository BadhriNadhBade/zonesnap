# Code of Conduct

Be kind and be useful.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who
break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through the
repository's Security tab (choose "Report a vulnerability" and say it is a
conduct report) or by opening a private discussion if enabled.
